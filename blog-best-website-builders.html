5 Best Website Builders in 2026: Which One Should You Use?

By Vixel Team|6 min read|March 2026

Whether you are a business owner building your first website or a freelancer trying to pick the right tool for clients, the number of options in 2026 is overwhelming. WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Framer, Hostinger, Webflow, custom code... the list goes on.

We have built websites on almost every platform. In this guide, we compare the 5 most relevant options for Indian businesses in 2026 and tell you honestly which one is right for your situation.

1. WordPress

WordPress powers over 40% of all websites on the internet. It has been around since 2003 and has the largest ecosystem of themes, plugins, and developers.

Pros

  • Massive plugin library (over 60,000 plugins for every feature imaginable)
  • Excellent SEO capabilities with plugins like Yoast and RankMath
  • Huge community for support and tutorials
  • Full ownership of your website and data
  • Highly customizable if you know what you are doing

Cons

  • Requires regular updates and maintenance (plugins, themes, WordPress core)
  • Security vulnerabilities if not maintained properly
  • Can be slow if you use heavy themes like Elementor with too many plugins
  • The learning curve is steeper than drag-and-drop builders
  • You need hosting separately, which adds complexity

Best for: Blogs, content-heavy websites, businesses that need a lot of functionality (e-commerce, membership sites, booking systems).

2. Wix

Wix is the most beginner-friendly website builder available. It uses a true drag-and-drop editor where you can move elements anywhere on the page without touching any code.

Pros

  • Extremely easy to use (no technical knowledge needed)
  • Good-looking templates out of the box
  • Hosting, SSL, and domain management included
  • Built-in features for booking, forms, and basic e-commerce

Cons

  • You do NOT own your website. It lives on Wix's servers and you cannot export it
  • SEO is decent but limited compared to WordPress or custom code
  • Sites can be slow, especially with many elements on a page
  • Monthly costs add up over time (uc0u8377 250 to u8377 800 per month forever)
  • Difficult to migrate to another platform later

Best for: Solopreneurs who want a simple online presence quickly and do not mind ongoing monthly costs.

3. Framer

Framer has become the darling of the web design world in 2025 and 2026. Originally a prototyping tool, it has evolved into a powerful website builder that produces clean, fast, beautifully designed websites.

Pros

  • Stunning design capabilities (far superior to Wix or WordPress templates)
  • Websites are fast because Framer generates clean, optimized code
  • Great free plan (1 project with Framer branding)
  • Built-in CMS for blogs and dynamic content
  • Excellent SEO controls (meta tags, Open Graph, schema support)
  • No coding needed, but gives you code-level control when you want it

Cons

  • Learning curve is steeper than Wix (not fully drag-and-drop)
  • Smaller plugin ecosystem compared to WordPress
  • Limited e-commerce features
  • Paid plans needed for custom domains and removing branding

Best for: Design-focused businesses, portfolios, agencies, and anyone who wants a modern, fast website without writing code.

4. Hostinger Website Builder

Hostinger's built-in website builder is one of the most affordable options available. It uses AI to generate a basic website in minutes based on your business type and preferences.

Pros

  • Very affordable (starts at around uc0u8377 150/month with hosting included)
  • AI website generation gets you started quickly
  • Simple, clean interface
  • Hosting, SSL, and domain included in the price

Cons

  • Limited design flexibility (websites tend to look similar)
  • Basic SEO tools (not as advanced as WordPress or Framer)
  • Limited integrations and third-party plugins
  • Not suitable for complex websites or businesses that need custom features

Best for: Absolute beginners on a tight budget who just need a basic online presence quickly.

5. Custom Code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)

This is what professional agencies (including Vixel) use for premium projects. The website is built from scratch, line by line, giving you complete control over every aspect of design, performance, and SEO.

Pros

  • Fastest loading websites possible (no bloated platform code)
  • Complete design freedom (no template limitations)
  • Best possible SEO performance (full control over every technical detail)
  • You fully own the code (host it anywhere, no platform lock-in)
  • Can be hosted for free on Vercel or Netlify
  • Most secure option (no plugins to hack, no platform vulnerabilities)

Cons

  • Requires a developer (you cannot do this yourself without coding knowledge)
  • Higher upfront cost compared to DIY builders
  • Content updates require developer help (unless you add a headless CMS)
  • Takes longer to build than using a template

Best for: Businesses that want a unique, high-performance website that stands out from competitors and ranks well on Google. Ideal when you hire a professional agency to build and maintain it.

Our Honest Verdict

If you are building it yourself with zero budget: Start with Framer (free plan) or Hostinger.

If you need a blog or content-heavy site: WordPress with good hosting.

If you want the best possible result for your business: Hire a professional agency that builds with custom code. The upfront cost is higher, but the long-term results in SEO, speed, and conversions make it the best investment.

The platform matters less than most people think. What matters more is the quality of design, the content, the SEO setup, and the ongoing maintenance. A beautifully built WordPress site will always outperform a poorly made custom-coded site.
